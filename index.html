Northbeam reads it

No typing, no sorting.

You don't fill in forms or pick categories. You send the thing as it is, and Northbeam takes it from there.

  1. Share it

    Forward a message, share a screenshot, snap a photo or record a voice note. Any language, any mess.

  2. It understands

    Dates, amounts, places, people and deadlines are pulled out and turned into events, reminders and to-dos.

  3. It follows through

    It nudges you before things are due, drafts the replies and keeps track of what's still open. You approve with one tap.

  • A photo of a bill or a fineA reminder three days before it's due, and a flag if the amount looks unusual.
