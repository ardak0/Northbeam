An AI assistant that helps small contractors find, understand and bid on public tenders in Türkiye.

Public tender documents are long and full of conditions. Small firms lose bids over a missed requirement, not because they weren't qualified. We're building a tool, powered by Claude, that does the reading for them.

What we're building

  • Tender monitoringSurface new tenders that match your trade, region and size.
  • Plain-language summariesTurn dense tender documents into the requirements, deadlines and risks that matter.
  • Eligibility checkCompare a tender's conditions against your company's profile and flag gaps early.
  • Bid checklistGenerate a step-by-step list of what to prepare and submit.

Status

In early development. Based in Istanbul.
